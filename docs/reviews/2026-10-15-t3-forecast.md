## Deviations
- `dayItems` is collected inside the existing `onApply` callback (not a second filter over `items`): it is the walk's own list by construction.
- `dayItems` is served in a stable order (day → income, expenses, bank rows → label / amount; ids break exact ties), not the
  walk's raw order: same-day rows came back in database row order, which a write changes (`chaseReviewMovesNoMoney`
  compares whole responses). No balance depends on the order inside a day.
- "Matched impact" stays, inside the disclosure (the figure was on the hero; kept for visibility).
- Day panel first day opens on `startingBalance` (was "—") so the identity holds on day one too.
- Date card and day panel: rust only below zero (T2's rule; they were rust under the buffer).
- `?day=` writes are debounced 250 ms on /forecast (hover-select would otherwise call `replaceState` per resting point).
- The deep link scrolls the day panel into view once (phone: the panel sits under the register).
- Kept T2's empty-card words ("No bank reading yet — the curve starts from your starting balance") over the design's
  "No bank balance yet · Link a bank" on the Checking today card; used the design's words on the date card's empty hint.
- D7 (dashboard HORIZONS) and D8 ("Near buffer") were already done by P2 / T1T2; T3 adds only the whitelist mirror test.
