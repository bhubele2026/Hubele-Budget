## The owner's decision

The plan's "Primary purpose": *control spending and get out of debt. Main screen: discretionary money until payday, within plan?, today's decision, debt actually paid down, next milestone.* S1 puts that on the open screen, from the position PR-B1 built.

- `artifacts/h2/src/screens/today/`: `Today.tsx` (composition + `TodayView`), `Hero.tsx`, `AssumptionsSheet.tsx` (lazy), `WeekSection.tsx`, `attention.ts` (pure order), `sections.tsx` (one thing, activity, coming up, debt), `WhatsNew.tsx` (lazy).

- `/design/today` (public, lazy, synthetic data, labelled "sample"): `screens/design/DesignToday.tsx`; `App.tsx`, `routePrefetch.ts`, `routes.test.tsx` in lockstep.

## Residuals

- "Reconnect <bank>": the spine does not carry an institution name, so the card says "Reconnect your bank".
- "N bills due this week": the spine carries the month's count only, so the foot reads "due this month".
- `GET /bills/summary` is the current month: at month end "due tomorrow" can miss next month's first bill.
- The weekly limit is the position's own `weekCap` first (override-aware, and what `remainingWeek` was measured against), then the shared plan, then settings.
- Ledger rows that do not count in the balance (a superseded pending row) are hidden, after the server's 8-row limit, so a window can show fewer than 8.
- "Existing household" = bank has reported, or spend this month, or ledger rows in the window.
- The `autoCategorize` switch is a preference only; the categorizer package reads it later.
- "Handled" (agent trail): left out until AI-1; marker is a TODO in `Today.tsx`, not in the UI.
