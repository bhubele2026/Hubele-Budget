## Imports — what the importer reads
- Route: `POST /api/import/workbook` (multipart `file`). The page accepts `.xlsx`; the parser (SheetJS) also reads `.xlsm`, `.xls`, `.ods`.
- Sheets: Debt Tracker, Monthly Budget, Recurring Items, Mapping, Payments (required); Monthly Tracking (optional); any other sheet ignored.
- Cells: amounts as numbers, "$1,234.56" text, blank or "—" (= 0); dates as date cells, Excel serial numbers, "YYYY-MM-DD" text, date-times; APR as 0.2499, 24.99 or "24.99%".
- No account column: every Payments row is the Amex register (`source "amex"`, no card account).

## Doubts for the owner
- A workbook import replaces every transaction of the importing user, bank rows included, and all categories, rules and debts. On a live household the card's Amex summary then reads $0.00. Restore brings it all back (now complete). Allow, warn or limit? Not changed.
- The restore is not offered on screen (the snapshot id is returned, unused). Not added.
- Text in a money cell now stops the import. A blank cell still imports as $0.00, including a Payments row with a date and no amount.
- A Target naming no budget category is ignored without a word.
- An empty model answer reads "Done · Filed 0 charges, N waiting for you." Honest about nothing filed; the status says Done.
- An Accept shows on Settings › Automation as "You · Filed · Corrected" (every hand filing is stored "corrected"); the model's row says "Accepted".
- An undone Accept goes back to unfiled, not back to the queue (S noted it; confirmed).
- "Checking covers $0 until Fri" (available until payday) sits beside "Checking today $2,985.00": different measures.
- Fixture only: after the first page visit the fixture's bank reads stale (fake token flagged needs-reconnect, no Plaid call), so its recap says "Bank data may be out of date".
- Monthly Tracking values keep the old reading (text → 0); free-form metrics, not money H2 computes with.
