## What the pages show
- `/spending/week` and `/spending/month` (routes, prefetch and nav unchanged).
- Header: "This week · Sun Oct 11 – Sat Oct 17" / "This month · October 2026". Another period reads "Week · …" / "Month · …".
- Scope line: "Household · weekly plan" / "Household · monthly plan".
- Prev / next arrows. Next stops at the current period. Prev stops at tracking start (2026-05-01).
- Summary: **Plan** · **Counts toward plan** · **Left** or **Over** (bad tone for Over).
- Plan not set: "Plan not set", Left reads "—". Never $0, never Over.
- "Why over?" (title "Where it went" when not over): categories largest first, share bar, "usual $Y", "above usual $Z" chip.
- Expand a category: date, merchant, account chip `name ••last4`, amount, Pending badge, plain reason ("Marked weekly", "Part 1 of 2 of $130.00").
- Each merchant links to its register via `txnRoute()` (with `tx`).
- "Not filed yet" group: "Counts toward the weekly plan until filed", rows, link to `/review/categories`. Empty for a month.
- Details (collapsed): counts toward plan, carried over, refund adjustment (if non-zero), "Beside the plan" (unplanned, the other allowance, matched to a bill, reimbursable, all household purchases) + one sentence on what the plan counts.
- Month only: separate **Household budget** block (planned, actual, "includes debt payments"), link to `/budget?month=`, and the dashboard's "Why these differ" popover (`SpendingWhy`, now exported, reused as is).

## Setting a plan (owner decision)
- Offered for the current period only, only when `plan.status = not_set`.
- Week: primary "Use $X as the weekly plan from this week", X = the `hook_stored` prefill. Other prefills as choices. "Different amount" opens the same dialog with an input.
- Month: no default primary; prefills (e.g. "Saved allowance") + "Different amount".
- Dialog: amount, start day (this Sunday / the 1st), "Past weeks keep their plan.", "Left or over … updates once it is saved."
- `POST /allowance-plans {period, amount, effectiveFrom}` only after Confirm. Cancel sends nothing.
- On success: plan list and period-breakdown queries invalidated; the app's mutation cache refreshes spine and position. Left or over comes from the refetch.
- A member (not owner) sees "Only the household owner sets the weekly plan."

## URL state
- `?start=YYYY-MM-DD` (period start; junk or future falls back to the current period).
- `?open=<categoryId>[,…]` (`unfiled` for Not filed yet, `none` for no category).
- Written with the existing `useUrlState` (replaceState), so a refresh, a shared link and Back from a register land on the same view.

## Deviations (code wins, intent kept)
- Share bar is `CssFillMeter` (value = category, ceiling = total counted), not `CssBars`: `CssBars` is a fixed-height absolute-positioned list and cannot hold an expanded row list under each category.
- `useCreateAllowancePlan`, `useGetPeriodBreakdown` and their keys come from `@workspace/api-client-react/features` (the import-graph test requires it for tagged operations).
- "Biggest charges" not added: it is inline JSX in `command-center.tsx` (not a component) and that file is off limits.
- Freshness line not added (no cheap shared source on the breakdown).
- Household budget shows Planned and Actual only; no left/over (it would be a browser subtraction).
