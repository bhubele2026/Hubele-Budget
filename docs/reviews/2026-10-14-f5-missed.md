# F5 — a Review answer never erases card purchases; a payment is used once; a card never owes more than its balance (branch fin2/f5-missed, from fin2/integration 8c80ed3a; rounds 1–6)

Server + avalanche-core + spec/codegen + web words. One migration (0210, round 3; additive). No setting written. Synthetic fixture only.

## The bug (round 1 diagnosis)
- "missed" / "skipped" / "dismissed" are the household's own Review answers, written by Mark missed / Skip (a plain row click too).
- F1 read any answer as "settled": the week left the obligations, was never paid, never carried.
- The household had answered every past Weekly Spend (to stop the pre-F1 stored $450 dragging).
- Result: every unpaid in_full card purchase dropped off the curve; every Amex payment "paid nothing".

## Owner's rules (binding) → where they live
- Every expected cash obligation once → core `allocateHookPayments` + `hookOccurrenceAmounts` (one carrier per hook item).
- Purchases on an in_full card stay owed until paid → an answer closes the RESERVE only (`reserveClosed`).
- Unpaid weeks carry forward → unchanged F1 carry; answered weeks carry and can be the carrier.
- Payments reduce obligations once → one pairing, one allocation, one `paymentAllocations` map per payment.
- Carried + current unpaid never above what the card owes → core `capHookObligationsAtBalance` (new).
- Transfers / card payments never counted as purchases → unchanged per-row rule (`cardRowOwed`).
- An answer closes a reserve, never erases purchases nor the rest of a payment → missed/skipped/dismissed and matched (below).
- Payoff modes: archived-debt card = in_full weekly; active-debt card = debt_plan (unchanged F1 defaults).

## What changed (round 1, kept)
- Missed / skipped / dismissed on a hook week (obligations model) → allowance left 0; purchases stay owed, paid by evidence, carried.
- The week's fate keeps the answer only when it owes nothing; otherwise carried / on the curve / paid on evidence, `answer` beside it.
- Card credits paired with their checking rows ONCE, before the matcher (core `pairCardPaymentCredits`).
- A row another card's ledger received never pays this debt's plan (`MatchRow.ledgerDebtIds`).
- Card-ledger minimum rule (core `minimumsPaidByCardLedger`), evidence `card_ledger`.
- Kill switch `HOOK_OBLIGATIONS=off`: pre-F1 model whole (every answer settles).

## What changed (round 2 — every HIGH / MEDIUM from the two reviews)
1. **Cap + leftover (HIGH).**
   - A payment pays the weeks closed on its date in full, then a later week only the purchases made by its date (`accruedByCents`). Its leftover is no longer dropped.
   - The open week is an obligation too (leftovers can pay its purchases already made).
   - Each in_full card's carried + current unpaid is capped at the card's live balance, oldest first (`cappedByBalance`, off the curve).
   - Cap = Amex anchor's answer for the card (`resolveAmexAnchor`), only when it is Plaid's live balance (`source: plaid`).
   - Rolled forward: + purchases − refunds dated after the day it was read, + pending purchases, − card credits after that day.
   - Less payments in flight: allocated checking rows with no card credit, pending or dated within 5 days before the read day.
   - A debt row's stored balance (an archived card's) is never used. No live balance, or one older than the rows read: no cap.
   - A cap only lowers what is reserved; it never moves a paydown onto one day.
2. **One payment, one obligation (HIGH).**
   - Pairing: issuer-name guard — the checking row must name the issuer, or be tagged to the card's debt; a row tagged to another debt never pairs.
   - Pairing window after the credit 2 → 5 days (weekend payments: card credit Friday, bank debit Monday).
   - A paired row is that card's: a credit paying a minimum consumes its checking row (never hook evidence).
3. **Matched answer (HIGH).** A matched row pays its week first (`forKey`), the rest pays the other weeks; purchases it did not cover stay owed (the week carries / drags). A matched answer with no row still settles.
4. **Card-ledger minimum window (MEDIUM).** Opens at a cautious statement close (due − 20 days), not the previous due date. A payment pays a second minimum only when dated after that minimum's close.
5. **Debt "paid down this month" (MEDIUM).** `loadConfirmedPayments` accepts `card_ledger`.
6. **Banking review, allocation-aware (MEDIUM).**
   - A payment row is compared with the SUM of what it paid (`ledger.paymentAllocations`): hook weeks and card-ledger minimums; the plan named is the one due nearest the row.
   - A confirmed (matched) row the same.
   - Planned list: a hook week's / card-ledger minimum's `txnAmount` = what was paid toward it, not the whole row.
   - `carried` weeks are listed, with `carriedTo` (the day they land on the forecast).
7. **Web words (LOW).**
   - Register / Missed bucket: a hook week whose purchases moved on reads **Carried** + "card purchases carried to Oct 15"; answered but still on the forecast reads **Still owed**. Never "Missed".
   - Missed bucket keeps those rows (Undo stays) but its count and total are the truly missed only.
   - A closed week with nothing left to spend says "card purchases $X still owed", not "reserve $300".
   - Part paid: "part paid by <row> · $X still owed" (register), "Part paid · $X still owed" (banking review).
   - A plain row click on a Weekly / Monthly Spend row no longer marks it missed; the explicit button does.

## What changed (round 3 — the two round-2 reviews: 3 medium, 3 low)
1. **The balance read, by the instant (MEDIUM).**
   - A card row is outside the balance when: pending now; pending at the read; stored after the read (`created_at` > the read); or dated after the read's day.
   - Core `outsideCardBalanceRead` + `cardOwedNowCents` (pure). The loader only gathers inputs.
   - "Pending at the read": the liability fetch now keeps the ids of the card's pending rows beside the balance (`plaid_accounts.liability_pending_txn_ids`, migration 0210).
   - Why the ids: Plaid's pending→posted re-key updates H2's own row in place (same id, `pending` flipped, dated the posting day, `created_at` unchanged). `created_at` alone misses that path.
   - A posted row that replaced a pending row reads its twin's status too (`replacedPendingId`, from the superseded-pending pairing). **Dropped in round 4** (a leftover pending copy put its twin outside at every read).
2. **Pending credits read like pending purchases (MEDIUM + LOW, same defect).**
   - `CardPaymentCredit` carries `pending`, `createdAt`, `replacedPendingId`; the same rule subtracts a credit outside the balance.
   - Plaid's current balance holds posted rows only (the available balance is the one that moves with pending ones), so both sides now read pending as outside.
3. **A mis-tagged payment is counted once (MEDIUM).**
   - Pairing pass 2 (`pairCardPaymentCredits`): a credit still alone takes a same-cents row in its window tagged to ANOTHER debt, once no credit on that debt's own card claimed it. Issuer-naming rows first, then the closest.
   - The matcher: when the card's ledger received a row and the tag names a debt not linked to that card, the ledger wins (`ledgerOverridesTag`): no `debt_tag` evidence, tier 3 for the tagged debt's plan.
   - A tag only ever says yes. A tag that agrees with the ledger (or with no ledger) decides as before.
4. **Monthly accrual (LOW).** `accruedByCents`: when the period's rows are known they decide, whatever the occurrence date. Without rows, the period's END (`HookObligation.periodEnd`), not the occurrence date.
5. **A payment before the window pays nothing in it (LOW).** `allocateHookPayments(…, { firstOccurrence })`: a payment dated before the earliest sized occurrence of its cadence pays nothing of that cadence (F1's rule). A matched answer still ties its row.

## What changed (round 4 — the two round-3 reviews: 2 high (one defect), 1 medium, 2 low)
1. **A leftover pending copy never moves its posted twin (HIGH).**
   - Round 3: the fetch kept every row still pending on the card, leftover copies too (the fixture's p8pending beside its posted twin p8).
   - Then `replacedPendingId` read p8 outside the balance at every read: the cap ran HIGH by a purchase, LOW by a payment credit.
   - Dropped the clause (core `outsideCardBalanceRead`) and the field from the card read (`amexAnchor.ts` back to round 2's superseded handling).
   - A twin stored after the read is outside by its store time; stored before, the balance holds it.
2. **Only a read right after a sync reads by the instant (MEDIUM + LOW, one cause).**
   - `fetchLiabilitiesForItem(userId, itemRowId, { afterTxnSync })`.
   - Only POST /plaid/sync passes it: after that item's transactions sync, not while the first batch is still preparing.
   - With it: the card's pending ids are kept beside the balance (`[]` when none).
   - Every other fetch (link, debt Refresh, liability-accounts refresh, create-debt): null, replacing an older list.
   - Core: null → the day decides alone (pending now, or dated after the read's day). Kept → also pending at the read, or stored after it.
   - So a credit the balance already held, stored after a debt's Refresh, is not subtracted again; a stale pending flag is never kept.
3. **Pass 2 keeps the issuer-name guard (LOW).**
   - A row tagged to another debt pairs with a card credit only when it names the issuer (a filter, not a rank).
   - A loan payment ("AUTO FINANCE PMT") tagged to the loan pays the loan's minimum, never a card's credit.
4. **Why a credit stored after a Sync's read is still subtracted.**
   - The review's minimum ask was "never subtract a credit by its store time". The main ask (only after a sync) is done instead.
   - A payment made after the morning Sync posts as a credit dated the read day, stored after the read.
   - By the day alone it reads inside the balance: H2 holds up to its amount over what the card owes until the next Sync (test: 300 held vs 290 owed).
   - The cost is in Doubts: a credit Plaid reports after a Sync's read, though the balance held it, reads the cap low.

## What changed (round 5 — the two round-4 reviews: 1 medium and 1 low from both, 1 low from one)
1. **Never over after a read that is not a Sync (MEDIUM, both).**
   - Round 4: on a read that kept no pending list (a debt's Refresh, a link, every read before the first Sync after deploy), the day decided alone.
   - So a payment made after that read came back: its card credit (dated the read day, stored later) read inside the balance; once it paired, its checking row left "in flight". H2 held up to the whole payment over what the card owes until the next Sync.
   - Now (core `creditOutsideCardBalanceRead`): on such a read, a PAYMENT CREDIT H2 stored after the read is outside the balance — subtracted once.
   - Only credits dated on or after the read's day − 5 (the in-flight window). Older ones stored late are history (a link, a backfill) the balance holds.
   - Purchases keep the day rule (read inside, a late purchase can only lower the cap).
   - Cost, accepted by the brief: a credit the balance already held but H2 stored after the read reads the cap LOW by it until the next Sync (allowed side).
   - No new Plaid call, no new sync.
2. **One payment, one obligation, whatever the bank's text (LOW, both).**
   - Pairing pass 2 (core `pairCardPaymentCredits`, new `opts.ledgerDebtIds`): a row tagged to another debt pairs with a card credit when it names the issuer OR its tagged debt is a card whose own ledger H2 reads (every roster card, `cardLedgerDebtIdsOf`).
   - Why that is evidence: had the tagged card received the row, pass 1 would have paired it there.
   - A debt with no ledger here (a loan, a card H2 does not read) still needs the issuer's name (round 4's loan case holds).
   - The pairing's name test takes the bank's abbreviations (core `paymentNamesCardIssuer`): "AMERICAN EXP CO", "AMER EXP", "AMEXEPAYMENT". Never "CAMEXICO", "AMERICAN EXPEDITIONS", "AMERICAN EXPORT".
   - Pairing only. The hooks' evidence rule for a row with no card credit keeps the strict name (`namesCardIssuer`): nothing corroborates it there.
3. **Sync of every item: each item's balance read right after its own sync (LOW, one review).**
   - `syncAllForUser(…, { onItemSynced })`; POST /plaid/sync reads that item's liabilities in it, not after the slowest item's poll (up to ~22 s).
   - Every synced item is still read once; a sync that does not report per item is read after it, as before.
   - Not done: saving null when an item's poll stopped while Plaid's refresh may still land (in Doubts).

## What changed (round 6 — the round-5 reviews: 2 medium, 4 low; four fixes, nothing else)
1. **Refunds read on the never-over side (LOW + MEDIUM: P2, A).**
   - Round 5: a refund stored after a non-Sync read kept the day rule: read inside, H2 held the refund over what the card owes.
   - Now core `cardOwedNowCents` reads a row with cents < 0 (a refund) through `creditOutsideCardBalanceRead`, as payment credits.
   - Purchases keep the day rule. Comments and the doubt fixed.
2. **Every read keeps its pending list (MEDIUM, both: P1, B).**
   - Round 5: a payment credit pending at a non-Sync read that posted in place later (same row, same store time) read inside: H2 held up to the whole payment over until the next Sync.
   - Every liability fetch now keeps the card's pending ids with the balance, plus `liability_pending_after_sync` (true only for POST /plaid/sync's read).
   - Migration 0210 extended (not merged anywhere yet): a second nullable column, `ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS`; drizzle schema matches.
   - Core: a non-Sync read's list is `CardBalanceRead.pendingAtOtherRead`. Read for payment credits and refunds only: pending at the read ⇒ outside, even after it posts in place.
   - A Sync's read is unchanged (`pendingAtRead`, every row by the instant). Purchases keep the day rule.
   - A stale flag (the balance already held a credit H2 still showed pending) reads the cap LOW until the next Sync: allowed.
3. **Pass 2 waits for the tagged card's window (LOW: P3, C).**
   - Round 5: a row tagged to a roster card's debt, still on its way, paired with another card's same-cents credit; the tagged in-full card then held it over (no week paid, not in flight).
   - Core `pairCardPaymentCredits(…, { todayISO })`: pass 2 takes a row tagged to a roster card's debt (named or not) only when dated on or before today − `CARD_CREDIT_PAIR_BEFORE` (5).
   - Both callers pass the household's today (forecast ledger, hooks loader). No today: never.
   - A row tagged to a debt with no ledger here (a loan) keeps round 4's issuer-name branch, not gated.
   - The round-5 mis-tag (row 10/8, today 10/14) still pairs.
4. **An abbreviated payment still on its way counts (LOW: P4).**
   - The hooks' unpaired-row evidence uses `paymentNamesCardIssuer` (abbreviations), not the strict `namesCardIssuer`.
   - "AMERICAN EXP CO PMT" / "AMER EXP PMT" with no card credit yet pays its week and counts in flight for the cap.
