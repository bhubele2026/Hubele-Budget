# Builder R: reports keep their period, Reports use the shared balance block (2026-10-17)

Branch `cons/r-reports` from `consistency/integration` c8ec3bee (= live 9c8d7d7f). Local commit only. NOT pushed, NOT deployed.
Fixtures only (rich2). No server file touched. No figure moved.

## What changed (plain words)
- A report now keeps the period you opened it with. The period lives in the address: `?mode=wk|mo|yr` or `?from=YYYY-MM-DD&to=YYYY-MM-DD`
- The hub tiles hand on the exact dates their figure used: "Last 30 days" opens the report on those 30 days, not on its own week
- With a custom span no Wk/Mo/Yr button is lit and the dates are shown ("Sep 16 – Oct 16"). Pressing Wk/Mo/Yr drops the span
- Reload, drilldown, Crumb and Back keep the period (walked, below)
- Budget report: the month picker is in the address too (`?month=1` = last month; this month is not written)
- The Reports "Bank balance" tile is gone. It is now the shared block: "Checking today", the figure, "Calculated · bank read $X <when> · +N rows since", Details
- The words "Plaid · <account>" and "Manual · <account>" are deleted. A typed balance reads "Typed balance $X (not from the bank) · <day>"; no reading reads "—" and "No bank reading yet"
- Before the spine answers the tile says "Loading…" (or "Couldn't load"); it never claims "No bank reading yet" early

## Deviations from the brief
- Budget tile does not carry a span: the Budget report is month-based and the tile's figure is a different measure. The month is URL state instead
- `useReportMonth` lives in `reportsShared.tsx` (not `useUrlState`) to keep the landing at +0
- Drilldown: the report pages have no navigating drilldown (the Recategorize list and weekday bars open in place). The journey opens the list, leaves by the Reports crumb, comes Back
- The Amex tile label was left as is
- Cash flow / Habits: no figure to tie (a sparkline, bars); the tie is asserted for Spending only
- `TimeRangeToggle` accepts `null` (no preset lit); a one-line type change in a shared component
