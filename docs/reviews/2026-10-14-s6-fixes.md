## 1. A return on a non-Amex credit card nets its purchase

### The bug (confirmed)
- `classifyRefund` called a credit "money back on a card" only on an Amex source (`amex`, `plaid:amex`).
- Every other card test (`isCardLedgerRow`) reads the Plaid account type (`credit`, any issuer).
- So a Chase / Capital One / Apple Card return with no REFUND word never offset its purchase: spending read high.

### The fix
- `classifyRefund(tx, ctx, opts)`: card test = `isCardLedgerRow(source, opts.isCardAccount ? "credit" : null)`.
  - `RefundOptions.isCardAccount` (new): the row's Plaid account is a credit account.
  - `onCardAccount(row, cardAccountIds)` (new, pure): reads it from the household's credit account ids.
- Payments stay out, unchanged (`isRefundCandidate`): payment words (`readsAsPayment`), issuer phrases, Plaid LOAN_PAYMENTS / TRANSFER / INCOME, the card-payment flag, debt rows, transfers.
- Checking unchanged: a credit there still needs a REFUND word.
- `loadCardAccountIds(householdId)` (new, `lib/cardAccounts.ts`): `plaid_accounts.account_id` where `type = 'credit'` — the categorizer guard's own account test, read once per request.
- Wired into every reader that nets refunds:
  - `buildSpendingFacts` → spine `spentWeek` / `spentMonth`, Spending facts.
  - `loadMoneyContext` → `MovementContext.cardAccountIds` (now **required**) → `classifyMovement` → position, period breakdown, Monthly Spend hook, ways back, banking review kinds, metrics, recap.
  - `reconcileMonthSpend` (Budget month's "why it differs") — `opts.cardAccountIds`, loaded in `routes/budget.ts`.
- Not changed, on purpose:
  - Amex payoff (`computeWeeklyPayoff`) and Amex summary: they read Amex rows only, whose source already says "card". Their answers are identical (full suite). `classifyCardCredit` accepts `isCardAccount` for a future non-Amex caller.
  - Categorizer refund queue (`heuristicStage`): see "Doubts" 2.

## 2. The review badge counts an unlinked pending/posted pair once

### The bug (confirmed)
- `computeReviewCount` counted both halves of a pair the sync never linked; spending drops the pending half.
- And a charge answered while pending was asked about again once it posted.

### The fix
- `computeReviewCount` reads spending's pairs: `findSupersededPendingForRange(month)`.
  - A replaced pending row is not counted.
  - The posted row carries the pending row's answers when it has none of its own.
- `rowAnswersResolve(own, replacedPending)` (new, avalanche-core `reviewStatus.ts`): that rule, pure.
  - The banking review's `unresolvedInRange` now calls it too (same rule, was inline; no number of its own moved).
- Spine `reviewCount` = GET /forecast/review-count = `computeReviewCount` (one call, parity green).

## Doubts / left undone
1. **A card payment with no payment words and no Plaid category** now reads as a refund on a non-Amex card and nets that card's purchases in its window (floored at 0 per account — spending could read low there).
   - E.g. an Apple Card payment "ACH DEPOSIT INTERNET TRANSFER…" with `pfc_detailed` null.
   - Plaid-synced rows carry `LOAN_PAYMENTS_CREDIT_CARD_PAYMENT` / `TRANSFER_IN_*`, which rule them out; manual or old rows without it are the exposure.
   - Amex has had the same exposure since B6. Not widened here (no new payment words).
2. **Categorizer refund queue left Amex-only.** Passing the card test to `heuristicStage` turned model-filed Chase-card credits into "Looks like a refund" questions (`categorizeModelStage` pins the model filing). That is a categorizer change, not a figure; left for the owner. Spending still nets those credits either way, and a filed one nets its category.
3. Amex payoff / summary do not pass `isCardAccount`. Identical today (their rows are `plaid:amex` / `amex`); a card whose rows carried another source would still read non-REFUND credits as `other_credit` there.
4. Ways back still count a split purchase whole under its own category; the breakdown splits it. Not in scope.
5. A trim's `usualWeek` is a signed median, like the breakdown's `usual`: a week whose refunds exceed its purchases counts negative. Not clamped, to agree.
6. One more query per review-count call (`findSupersededPendingForRange`; a single indexed probe when no pending row is in reach).
7. Test hermeticity (pre-existing): `categorizeModelStage` inserts a fixed-id account (`acct-ai1-card`, `onConflictDoNothing`); a failed run leaves it behind and the next run fails. Cleaned in my DBs; not changed.
