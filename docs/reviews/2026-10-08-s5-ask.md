## Ask a question: what happens

1. The person types (or taps a suggestion, which only fills the box) and presses Ask or Enter.
2. With no conversation open, `POST /ai/conversations` makes one.
3. The browser opens `POST /api/ai/chat` with the conversation id, the text and `userAskedToChange` (true only if the box was ticked).
4. The question shows at once. While the server reads, a quiet line says what it is looking at; answer text streams in as plain text.
5. `done` arrives with the server's checked text, which replaces the streamed words. The thread is refetched and the stored pair takes over; proposals and the conversation list are refetched. If the box was ticked the spine and ledger are marked stale.
6. `ref:<id>` in the answer becomes a link to `/activity?txn=<id>`; a proposal made by this run shows as a card under the answer.
7. An `error` frame shows the server's own words (budget: "Ask has reached its limit for now." with a link to AI cost). A dropped stream is polled every 2 s (up to 30 tries) until the stored answer exists.
8. AI off (`healthz.ai.enabled` false): the composer is disabled with "Ask needs AI turned on — Household › AI".

`useHealthCheck`, `useListAiConversations`, `useGetAiConversation`, `useCreateAiConversation`, `useListAgentProposals` (`proposed`, and `all` limit 50), `useApproveAgentProposal`, `useRejectAgentProposal`, `useListMemory`, `usePutMemory`, `useDeleteMemory`, `useListWishlist`, `useCreateWishlistItem`, `useUpdateWishlistItem`, `useGetAiUsageSummary`, `useUpdateAiBudget`, `useGetMe`, `useListCategories`, `useSpine`; `getAiConversation` for the poll. Memory, wish-list and budget writes carry `OWN_INVALIDATION`; proposal approval uses the central spine invalidation (it can move a plan).

## Laws kept

- No money computed in the browser; answer text is plain React text, never HTML (test with markup in an answer).
- Approve always asks first; the server applies it. A 409 or 403 says nothing changed.
- `userAskedToChange` is never inferred; the box resets after each message.
- No new dependencies. Frames are validated by a small guard in `aiStream.ts`, not by importing `@workspace/api-zod` (h2 does not depend on it). `aiStream.test.ts` holds the guard to the generated `AiChatResponse` on a spread of valid and invalid events so they cannot drift.
