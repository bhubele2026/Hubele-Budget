## Marker legend
| Kind | Shape | Token |
|---|---|---|
| Payday (money in) | up-tick triangle | navy |
| Bill | dot | steel |
| Card payment (name says Amex/card/Visa...) | square | Amex teal |
| Debt payment (recurring item linked to a debt) | diamond | orange |

One marker per day, on that day's balance, drawn in the kind of the day's largest event; more than one event shows a count. Horizons with more than 80 event days keep the 80 largest. Risk tints: under the buffer (light), below zero (strong). A "Today" rule and a zero line (only when the axis goes negative) are drawn.

## How selection stays stable
- `selectedDate` (ISO string) lives in `NextForecastPage`, above the data page, so refetch, horizon change and tab change never reset it.
- The chart and panel look the day up by date. If the date leaves the window the panel keeps it and says "outside this window"; the close shows a dash.
- Click or tap selects; on a fine pointer, resting 140 ms selects. Left/right move the day (outside the window, Right goes to the first day and Left to the last), Escape clears.
- Draw animation: plays for a new content fingerprint, keeps running through unrelated renders until it has had its time, then stays off. A horizon change re-keys the wrapper, which crossfades via `chart-in`. Reduced motion is honoured by `ANIM_AREA`/CSS.

## Selected-day actions
Mark missed and Skip (past-due carried events, hidden for partly-paid plans) call the existing handlers. "Match, move or part-pay in register" switches to the register tab and runs `jumpToPlan`, where the existing row actions (Match-to, suggested match, reschedule, partial) live. Those three are not duplicated in the panel.
