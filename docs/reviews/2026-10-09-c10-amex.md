## D3 — card scope lost on a cold load (fixed here)

- **Before:** the "selected card no longer on the page" fallback ran while the options were still empty, during a cold load. It reset the filter to "All cards".
  - The embedded effect only watched `[embedded, accountId]`, so it never ran again.
  - Result: `/next/accounts/:id` and `?accountId=` showed every card.
- **Now:**
  - the fallback waits until the month's rows and the linked items have both answered;
  - it never fires embedded (the route chose the card);
  - a card that is truly absent still falls back to All cards.
- **Tests:** `amexLayout.test.tsx` "D3":
  - embedded cold load, and `?accountId=` cold load: both fail on the old effect (mutation-checked) and pass now;
  - the absent-card fallback still works.

## Findings for the lead

- **Fixed in this branch, with your OK:** the card Summary on `/next/accounts/:id` showed "Statement balance" and "This week's charges" as dashes.
  - Cause: B3 matched the weekly-payoff card with `c.plaidAccountId === selected.plaidAccountId`. The payoff card's `plaidAccountId` is the INTERNAL row id (`lib/amexAnchor.ts:221`); the route's is the external account_id.
  - Now `payoffCardFor` (`Accounts.tsx`) matches on either id the items response exposes: `accountId` = external, or `plaidAccountId` = the entry's row id. That is the same either-id rule the route uses.
  - `Accounts.test`:
    - the seed now has the API's shape;
    - a match on each id shows the statement balance, this week's charges and the charge count;
    - another card's entry lends nothing.
    - Three tests fail on the old match and pass now.
- **Embedded band:** it still offers "All cards" and the other cards inside one card's account page, as B3 left it. The account selector above is the real switcher. Hiding the band's other cards when embedded is a product call.
- **AX-16…18** (override markers, external-card chip, matched-rule link) remain phone-only, per the parity doc's open owner question.
