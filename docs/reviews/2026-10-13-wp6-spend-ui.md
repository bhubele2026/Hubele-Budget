## What changed
- **Spending panel labels:**
  - The budget bar reads "This month vs budget · Oct 1–31 · everything filed to a budgeted expense category, every account (card and debt payments count here)".
  - The second total reads "Purchases so far $X · Oct 1–today · card payments, debt payments, transfers and reimbursables left out · refunds netted". "$Y this week" stays.
- **Bridge line:** "Budget counts $X of card payments and $Y of debt payments that purchases leave out; refunds net $Z". It reads the budget month's `spendingReconciliation` and is hidden when that is null.
- **"Why these differ" popover:**
  - It uses the "Why this number?" shell and makes no request of its own.
  - It shows Budget, Purchases and the difference, then the server's non-zero terms with their signs (the two uncategorized terms subtract).
  - When `unexplained` is not zero it says "$X of the difference is not explained by these lines".
  - It links to /reports/spending.
- **Findings:** `findingTitle(f, {categoryNameOf})` (`lib/agentTrail.ts`) gives "<Category> spending is speeding up". FindingsList has a `categoryNameOf` prop, and AttentionPanel passes the loaded categories.
- **One source for warnings:** `attentionItems` gains a `short` item from `lowPointView` (under the buffer, or below zero). It reads "The forecast runs short · Low $X on <day> · <words>" → /forecast.
  - The header action (`headerActionOf`) and Needs attention read that same item. The separate `runsShort` input is gone.
  - The header's facts line drops "N charges to match", which Needs attention's own row already says.
- **Archived wording (live check):** an archived card that still carries a balance reads "Archived · not on the payoff plan". The Debts page chip says "Archived". The plan words wait for Plaid's figures. WP3c refines this into one shared decision.
