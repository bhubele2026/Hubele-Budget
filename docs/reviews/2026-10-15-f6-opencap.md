# F6 — the card cap must include the open week (branch fix/f6-opencap, from origin/main 5e2480e1)

Server + avalanche-core scope. Synthetic fixture only. No live amounts in this note.

## Finding
- The fix the brief asks for is already the shipped behaviour (F5 round 2, live 5e2480e1).
- `loadHookPayoffs` puts every STARTED occurrence in `obligations`, the open week too (`everydayHooks.ts`, the obligations loop: `p.start <= todayISO`).
- The cap is applied to all of them, per in_full card, oldest first: `capHookObligationsAtBalance(obligations, allocations, balanceCaps)`. Nothing filters to due.
- The open week is cut last, so it keeps its purchases; `allowanceLeft` is never in the cap (`hookOccurrenceAmounts`).
- Proof on the base, no source change:
  - the brief's fixture (below) passes as is: 6 of 6;
  - a scratch due-only cap (`obligations.filter(due)`, the brief's model of the bug) fails 4 of the 6 new tests and 19 F5 `missedAnswers` cap tests; restored after the run.
- So the live gap cannot come from the open week escaping the cap.

## What the live gap must come from instead (for the lead; read-only checks)
- With the open week in the cap, the curve holds more than "balance + this week's pending" only if:
  1. no cap was applied to the card, or
  2. owed-now (`cardOwedNowCents`) read higher than "balance + this week's pending".
- Had the cap read "balance + this week's pending", the live code would have cut the oldest carried weeks and shown `cappedByBalance` on them.
- (1) No cap — `everydayHooks.ts`, the cap block skips a card when:
  - `resolveAmexAnchor(…, card)` is not `plaid`: the card's `plaid_accounts` row needs `type = 'credit'`, `liability_kind` null or `credit`, `liability_balance` set;
  - the read's household day is before `cardRead.readFrom`.
- (2) Owed-now too high — rows read OUTSIDE the balance are added:
  - pending now; dated after the read's day;
  - only after a Sync's read (`liability_pending_after_sync = true`): pending at the read, or stored after it.
- Suspects for (2):
  - rows H2 still shows pending that Plaid had already posted into the balance (F5's open "over" doubt);
  - a kept pending row Plaid removed (a person touched it: `plaid_removed_at`) beside its posted twin, not paired as superseded — counted in its week AND added to the cap, so the cap cannot see it;
  - after a Sync's read, OLDER purchases H2 stored later (a re-link, a backfill): purchases have no date guard (round 5's in-flight window covers credits only).
- Check (read-only): the card's `plaid_accounts` row (the four fields above + `liability_last_fetched_at`, `liability_pending_txn_ids`); the sum of its in-window purchase rows that read outside by the rules above; any pending row beside a posted twin.

# Round 2 — why the live card showed no cut (the lead's read-only evidence)

## The lead's question
- Live: the per-card anchor answers `plaid` (read Wed 15:11 Central, before 0210 shipped: no pending list, no Sync flag).
- The card's debt row is ARCHIVED and linked; payoff mode in_full.
- The hooks held carried due weeks + this week ABOVE the balance + the purchases pending 10/04–10/17; no week read `cappedByBalance`.
- Suspects named: the id form the cap passes, the owner argument, a debt-row anchor for a debt-linked card, `readFrom` vs the read's day, an early `continue`.

## The suspects, checked (none applies)
- Id form: the obligations, the roster, the modes and the per-card anchor all use the EXTERNAL Plaid `account_id` (`discoverAmexCards` → `loadCardPayoffModes` → `obligations` → `resolveAmexAnchor(…, accountId)`), as the route does.
- Owner argument: the anchor's `plaid` branch reads nothing by owner (household + account only).
- Archived debt row: the `plaid` branch answers before the debt branch; the debt row is never read when Plaid has a balance.
- `readFrom`: the earliest bound of the card read (the first sized week's start, or a minimum's statement close), weeks before the read.
- Early `continue`: only "not plaid / no finite balance" and "read before the rows" — neither holds.
- Same function, same arguments as `GET /amex/anchor` → the cap path gets the same `plaid` answer, so the card HAS a cap entry.
- Pinned: test (A) — the live row shape (credit liability, read before 0210, archived linked debt with an old balance): the anchor is `plaid` under any owner argument; the cap cuts.

## So owed-now read at least what was held
- No week cut ⇒ `cardOwedNowCents` ≥ everything held for the card.
- On a read before 0210 the day decides: a purchase is added when pending now, or dated after the read's day.
- Pending 10/04–10/17 plus every purchase dated after the read's day (all in the open week) stay below what was held.
- The rest can only be purchases dated BEFORE 10/04 that H2 still shows pending: weeks-old holds.
- Each such row counts in its week AND lifts owed-now by the same amount: the cap rises with the weeks and never cuts.
- Likely source: a bank hold that never posted, kept by H2 because the household had filed or flagged it (`plaid_removed_at`, the PR-A keep rule); or a pending copy whose posted twin the pairing missed.
- Read-only confirmation for the lead: the card's rows with `pending = true` dated before 10/04 back to the window's start (mid-August), with `plaid_removed_at`.

## The fix (core, minimal)
- New `purchaseOutsideCardBalanceRead` (`lib/avalanche-core/src/everydayHooks.ts`), used by `cardOwedNowCents` for purchases.
- A purchase dated more than `SUPERSEDE_MAX_DAYS` (7, H2's longest pending → posted gap) before the read's day is INSIDE the balance for the cap — posted into it, or never owed.
- Covers both: a stale pending hold, and an old purchase stored after a Sync's read (a re-link, a backfill).
- Only lowers the cap. Credits and refunds keep their own rule (a stale pending credit is still subtracted: never over).
- Not changed: the weeks themselves (a stale hold still counts in its week; the cap now bounds the total).

# Round 3 — expired holds count nowhere (owner's decision 2026-10-15)

## The rule
- "A charge still pending more than 7 days after its date: stop counting it everywhere — worked out, nothing deleted. If it posts later, the posted charge counts."
- Expired hold: pending now, on a Plaid account, dated more than 7 days before the household's today (Chicago), not replaced by a posted row. Any sign. Manual rows never.
- Its reversal goes with it, even when younger: pending, opposite sign, same account, same amount to the cent, dated on the hold's day to 7 days after.
- Lead's amendment: no description test for the reversal. Ranking among candidates: fuzzy-equal description, closest date, oldest, id. One to one; holds oldest first, then id.
- Read time only. No sync change, no row written, no migration.

## Where it lives (one answer, one place)
- Core rule: `expiredHolds(rows, today, replacedIds)` + `isExpiredByAge` (`lib/avalanche-core/src/pendingSupersede.ts`).
- Reader helpers (core, pure): `countsNowhere(s, id)`, `notCountedIdsOf(s)` = replaced ∪ expired.
- Server: `findSupersededPending` / `findSupersededPendingForRange` now return `expiredIds` beside `replacedIds` (`supersededPending.ts`); `{ today }` injectable.
- Range read = the whole ledger's answer for the range:
  - rows dated before today − 7: expired iff pending, Plaid, not replaced — no pairing;
  - younger rows: only as a reversal, so the pairing starts at a clean cut (no possible ± pair crosses it) on or before the earliest hold that can reach the range, and runs forward to yesterday (a hold prefers a fuzzy-equal reversal, which can sit after `to`);
  - the replaced pairs are read over that same window, once.
- Cash rule (core `classifyCashRows`): new reason `expired` (adds 0), computed by the same core rule over the rows the ledger reads.
  - One exception: a reversal whose hold the bank snapshot holds still counts — `available` held the hold, the reversal is the money coming back (before-the-rule cash).
- Card cap: round 2's read-day rule (`purchaseOutsideCardBalanceRead`) removed. Expired holds and reversals never reach `cardOwedNowCents` (`readCardPeriods` drops them). Posted rows keep the base rule (fixes finding 3).
- Kill switch: `EXPIRED_HOLDS_RULE=off` (`expiredHoldsRuleOn`, read per call): `expiredIds` empty, cash rule run without it = base 5e2480e1 exactly. The ledger share key carries it.
