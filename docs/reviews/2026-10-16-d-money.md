## What the trace found about the "double count"
- On both shapes the old reading kept the hook's TOTAL right: a claimed card payment paid only through the partial (N); an allowance purchase was dropped from "spent" by the answer and taken off the reserve (P). The money was misplaced (a payment credited to a future month), not doubled.
- The old reading read HIGH only when the partial named a row that is neither a card payment nor allowance spend (a bill's row): it "paid" the card with it. The guard removes that.
- ⚠️ The guard's limit (pinned as a test): in P the spending classifier (not changed) still reads the stored answer and leaves the row out of October's spent; the ledger no longer takes it off. The forecast reads $45.50 LOWER than with no answer — never higher (owner's rule: may read low, never high). Fully consistent needs the classifier to ignore hook partials too — that moves Spending figures; not done.
