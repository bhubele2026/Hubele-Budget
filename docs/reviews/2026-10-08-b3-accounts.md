# B3 — the account experience at `/next/accounts/:plaidAccountId` (2026-10-08)

Branch `restore/b3-accounts`, base `8d07020b`. UI only: no API, money maths or generated-client change.

## Approach
The Amex and Chase pages are 2,400 and 2,900 line components that own their state. They were MOVED IN WHOLE, not rewritten: each gained two optional props and nothing else changed.
- `AmexPage({ embedded?, accountId? })`: opens on one card (external Plaid account_id); `embedded` drops the page title (Sync and Connect stay) and the negative sticky margins.
- `TransactionsPage({ embedded?, accountKey? })`: opens on one linked account (internal row id); same `embedded` rule.
- With no props both render exactly as before; `/amex` and `/transactions` and all their tests are untouched and green.
- The new page lazy-loads them (`React.lazy`), so nothing joins the landing bundle.

## Blank because the API lacks it
- Card due date: only `dueDay` exists (shown "14th of the month"); no full due date.
- Statement balance: only on the weekly-payoff card; blank for cards not in that response.
- Minimum payment and current balance: only from a linked debt; blank (never zero) when no debt matches the account.
- Checking "ending balance": not a field; it is computed inside the embedded Chase ledger.
- Savings, loans, other: no activity view (the embedded pages are card and checking only).

## Not done / follow-ups
- Rows inside the embedded ledgers keep their own row design (not `TxnTable`); each ledger is single-account, so identity is the page's selector and summary. The combined view's `TxnTable` shows the short label with the full label in the tooltip (B0's primitive).
- A non-Amex credit card opens the Amex ledger; its source filter (`AMEX_SOURCES`) may hide that card's rows. Needs a follow-up in the Amex page's scope.
- Playwright pass for the embedded layout not run (no live data locally).
