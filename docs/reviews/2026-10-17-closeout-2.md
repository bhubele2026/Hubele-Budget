## 1. Report percentages agree — FIXED, verified on fixtures
- Cause: the hub tile divided each category by the sum of the five categories it showed; the report divided by
  categorized spending only (the server's `pctOfRealSpend`), and neither counted uncategorized spending. Same amount,
  two denominators, two percentages.
- Fix: one shared calculation (`reportsAnalytics` share helpers, re-exported as `lib/spendShare`) with one declared
  denominator — all spending in the span, uncategorized included as its own explicit row and slice — used by the tile
  legend and bar, the report's top-category stat, the pie legend, "Other", and the tooltip; the denominator is named in
  words on both surfaces ("% of $X spent <dates>"). Zero total prints "—", never NaN or 100%. Refunds keep the existing
  netting rule, said in the help text. The "Total real spend" headline (categorized only) is unchanged and labelled.
- Evidence: `spendShare.test.ts` (10), `spendShareAgreement.test.tsx` (3: same amount, percent, denominator words and
  dates on the tile and the report, with an uncategorized remainder, and the no-uncategorized case); journey P1 in
  chromium + webkit at 1280 + 390; period routing tests and journeys R1–R5 still green.
- Owner note: the Uncategorized slice shares a colour family with the seventh category slot.

## 2. Workbook import has a safe, usable recovery flow — FIXED, verified on fixtures
- Documented behaviour before the change: an import replaced every transaction of the importing person (bank rows
  included), budget lines and months, recurring items, mapping rules, monthly snapshots, debts and categories, with no
  preview and no confirmation; the snapshot was taken but never shown; restore replayed it blindly and silently
  discarded anything done since.
- Now: upload only previews (`POST /import/workbook/preview`, a read-only transaction) and lists adds, replaces (bank
  rows called out with their day span), kept rules, and every file problem by sheet, row and field; the real import
  needs an explicit confirm token (person + file hash + what it replaces) and answers 428 without it, 409 if anything
  changed since the preview; a complete snapshot is kept and named; Settings › Data › Workbook import shows History
  with Restore; a restore preview says what it puts back and removes; a restore that would lose later edits is
  **blocked** with the reason and counts (page-load writes excluded); repeated restore 409, two at once serialized,
  another person 404; failed imports and failed restores leave nothing; snapshots from before this change are refused
  as "Too old to restore". Short messages with the next action.
- Evidence: `importRecovery.integration.test.ts` (24), `importWorkflow.integration.test.ts` (22, updated),
  `WorkbookImport.test.tsx` (13), words and toast tests; 8 of 8 mutation checks turn the suites red; journey W
  (bad file → preview → Replace → History → blocked → explained → Restore → the dashboard's 11 figures back to the
  pre-import values) in chromium + webkit at 1280 + 390.
- Owner choices: keep restore strict (blocked by any later edit) or allow keeping later bank rows; refuse imports that
  would replace bank rows, or keep the warning.
