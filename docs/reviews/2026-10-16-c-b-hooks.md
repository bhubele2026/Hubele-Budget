## What changed (plain words)
- A Weekly / Monthly Spend row on the Forecast now says exactly what the forecast takes for it.
- The words add up to that figure to the cent, or name what else moved it (a part payment, money already paid).
- A moved or dragged row reads its own week or month and says so: "October's Monthly Spend, due Nov 1: …".
- When the row shows the item's stored amount but the forecast takes another, the words say both.
- A moved row no longer borrows the answer of the row whose day it moved onto.
- "Set a weekly plan →" on a Forecast row the forecast holds at the item's amount (owner); members read "The owner sets the weekly plan".
- Spending's plan prompt says, in one sentence, how the forecast's reserve and the plan relate.
- `/spending/week?plan=1` opens the plan dialog on the same choices; nothing is picked or saved until Confirm.
- Allowances' weekly plan panel offers "Set a weekly plan →" when no plan exists (members: "Plan not set").
- After a plan is set, the Forecast reads it at once (the cash signal is now marked stale by every write).
- The twin-accounts test dates its rows on the household's day; it no longer fails after 7 PM Central.

## C4 — the three causes, traced in the code (d8da19c4)
Register row amount (fact found): `PlanDropRow` shows the BUNDLE event = the item's stored amount (or a partial's remainder of it), not the curve event. The words describe the curve. (Brief's premise said the row amount is the curve event; it is not on the register.)

| Cause | Could the code produce it? | Now closed by |
|---|---|---|
| (a) partial remainder, words ignore the paid part | Yes in the ledger (`planAmount = base − paid`, forecastLedger.ts:1776). No screen writes a partial on a hook row today (hooks never get a server pair; the bank-card chips write "matched"); only legacy data or an API write | Words: "· $85.00 paid by <row> · $535.00 still owed"; the partial is read from the register's own line (`hookPayoffsOf(…, register.allPlan)`) |
| (b) words looked up under another key | Yes. `PlannedItemsList` used `itemId|row.date` (the shown day). "Move to…" is offered on hook rows; a monthly hook on the 1st moved to the next 1st read the next month's reserve. Worse: `buildLineRegister` let the moved row borrow the other occurrence's answer (its partial) | `hookLineOf` (occurrence key: `originalDate ?? date`) in the register and the Missed bucket; event keys `occurrenceKey ?? itemId|occurrenceDate`; `buildLineRegister` honours a moved-day answer only when no occurrence owns that day (the server's `closedAtMovedDate` rule); the words name the period |
| (c) a part the words skip | Yes, with no plan: "reserve $X" printed whenever the curve was X less a payment, plus carried, less the card's balance cut (a test even pinned "reserve $380" over a $120 event). With a plan the parts already summed (paid / capped are out of `incurred`) but were never named | Parts printed for both bases; "· $X already paid" and "· $X paid per the card's balance" named; a guard never prints parts that do not add up (then only "$X on the forecast") |
| (d) found: the row's own amount is the stored amount | Yes, on rich2 itself: Monthly Spend Oct 28 shows −$400.00 under "card purchases $0.00 + left to spend $30.80" (curve −$30.80) | "… = $30.80 on the forecast (item amount $400 not used)"; the face is unchanged (WP8 open decision, see Doubts) |
