# W4: "Same with Amex" — the Amex page as a review destination

Branch `fix/w4-amex`, from `fix/nav-review` (3bd17e63). Web only. No API, spec, schema or codegen change.

## What changed
- **Selector:** the card band lists every card, debt-linked ones too, as `name ••last4` (wraps, never truncates the last four). Debt-linked tile says "On the payoff plan". The section tabs list them too.
- **Summary first** (`GET /amex/summary`, card or all, period from the month/week controls):
  - Owed (current + statement; "—" and "Not known yet" when unknown) · Purchases · Payments · Credits & refunds (other credits on a small line).
  - Plan line by `planLink.kind`: weekly hook "Saturday payoff $X" → Forecast; monthly hook likewise; debt "On the payoff plan: minimum $X" → Debt; none → no line.
  - Household line: "Adds $X to this week's household plan" → `/spending/week` (Month/Year: weekly and monthly shown apart).
  - Purchases, Payments, Credits each filter the activity below; click again to clear.
- **Needs attention** (before the editor): Unplanned · Not filed yet · No category, count and amount, rows. A row jumps to Month mode on its month, clears filters, scrolls to the row, marks it, writes `?tx=`. It pushes its own history entry, so Back returns.
- **Activity editor:** unchanged, below. Bucket marks are W3's, reused as is.
- **Chart:** "Balance history · last 12 months", series named "Actual balance". Window = the 12 months ending this month, ending at today (no future area). Fixes the window pinned to May 2026 (ran out April 2027). A month with no balance now skips its weeks (was: stop).
- **Dashboard Amex tile:** "Purchases this week $X" (all cards, this week). Its own lazy chunk, asked after first paint; nothing while loading, failed or absent.
