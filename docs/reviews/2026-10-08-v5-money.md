  new writer or the ways-back maths (a test proves it).

## The owner's decisions this package rests on

- **The forecast may read low, never high** (2026-09-15). An adjustment can only LOWER a week: the amount is whole
  cents and strictly negative — refused by the route, the writer and a table CHECK; `computePosition` throws on a
  positive one.
- **The household chooses; nothing else does.** A carry-over is written only by the household's owner, through one
  writer. No job, no agent tool, no model.
- **% paid, never the amount owed** on the landing. The spine's two new debt words are amounts PAID and CHARGED this
  month — never a balance, never available credit (the spine law test walks every key).
- **Code computes every figure** (CLAUDE.md §1).

## Definitions

**Ways back** (`GET /money/ways-back`; every amount whole cents):

- `overBy = max(0, −remainingWeek)` — 0 when not over or no cap.
- `daysLeft` = days left in the Sunday–Saturday week, today included (1–7).
- `hold.perDay = floor(max(0, remainingWeek) ÷ daysLeft)` — rounded down; null with no cap.
  `hold.leavesUntilPayday = availableUntilPayday` (null with no bank data — never a false zero).
- `trims` = the 3 categories that spent most this week among rows that **count against the cap**
  (`allowance_weekly`, `needs_classification` — the rows `spentWeekDiscretionary` sums) and carry a category; ties by
  name, then id. Unplanned and monthly-allowance spend sit beside the cap and are never trims; uncategorized rows have
  nothing to name. `usualWeek` = median of the category's spend in each of the 8 weeks before this one (a week with none
  is 0; weeks starting before `TRACKING_START` are left out; an even count takes the lower-rounded mean of the middle
  two; null when no week counts).
- `carryOver.nextWeekCap = max(0, next week's cap + adjustment)`, where the adjustment is the row already applied to next
  week, or `−overBy` when none is; null with no cap next week. `applied` says whether a row exists.

**Week adjustments:** `POST` upserts on (household, week, `carry_over`): `amountCents` a negative integer ≥ −10,000,000;
`weekStart` a real Sunday, this household week or later; `reason` ≤ 200 chars; unknown keys refused. `DELETE` → 204, or
404 when the household has no row for that week. Members: 403 `owner_only`.

**`debt.confirmedPaymentsMtd`** = the gross of `loadConfirmedPayments` over the household month to today (transfer
pairs included) = `GET /debt-plan .confirmedMtd`.

**`debt.newChargesMtd`** — a transaction row counts, at its posted amount, when all hold:
1. the household's, dated in the household month to today, posted (not pending), not `is_transfer`, not a logged
   payment claim (`payment_state` null);
2. on a debt's own account: (a) a Plaid `credit`/`loan` account a debt links; (b) a `credit`/`loan` account no debt
   links, where the row itself is tagged to a debt (`debt_id`); (c) workbook `amex` rows when the household tracks a
   manual Amex debt (the liability ledger's rule). A debt-tagged row anywhere else (checking, a claim) is a payment by
   construction; an untagged row on an unlinked card is tied to no debt — both left out;
3. `classifyLiabilityRow` reads it `charge` — interest, fees, payments and refunds are out; refunds are not netted;
4. not half of a balance transfer: `pairTransfers` (±5 days, ±1%, a different debt, one to one) against payment rows
   on the debts' accounts and the confirmed bank payments.
When unsure, a row is left out: it may read low, never high.

## Residuals

1. **⚠️ Deploy order: `0115` must run before this code serves.** `loadPositionInputs` reads `plan_adjustments` on every
   `/money/position`, `/spine`, afford and ways-back request; without the table they fail. The boot runner
   (`MIGRATE_ON_BOOT`) / Pre-Deploy Command applies it first.
2. **Spine cost:** one indexed SELECT on `plan_adjustments` (this and next week) and, for `newChargesMtd`, the
   household's credit/loan accounts, its debts and up to a month (+5 days) of rows on those accounts. Not measured on
   production data.
3. **`paceAllowedToday` stays on the cap** (unadjusted), and `tight` compares the adjusted remaining with the cap's even
   share — so `tight` reads earlier, never later, but the pace figure itself reads the unadjusted pace (higher) in an
   adjusted week. Kept so only `remainingWeek` / `safeToSpendNow` move (Question 1).
4. **The usual week classifies older rows with today's context.** The ledger's tier-2 bill pairs cover only the
   ledger's window, so an older bill payment with a category and no confirmed match can read as unfiled spend in its
   week and lift that category's usual week. Informational; no cash figure depends on it.
5. **`newChargesMtd` reads low where unsure:** untagged rows on an unlinked card, fees, and a purchase that happens to
   match a payment to another debt (±1%, ±5 days) are left out; refunds are not netted. Balance-transfer pairing here
   reads transaction rows, while `genuinePaidDown` pairs with `debt_ledger_events` — a draw recorded only as a ledger
   event lowers `paidDownMtd` but is no new charge (the debt-words fixture shows it).
6. **The agent can see a carry-over** (`get_position` reads the position, which now carries `weekAdjustment`); it
   cannot make one. Read-only by design.
7. A carry-over may be set for any future Sunday (no upper bound), and DELETE accepts any week, past ones included.
8. No h2 screen uses the new routes yet (out of scope); the Today test mock was patched for the new required types.
9. `pnpm audit --prod` exits 0 with one high advisory the workspace already ignores (`pnpm-workspace.yaml`
   `ignoreGhsas`); nothing added here.
10. Commits carry `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5` (the builder's model, per the session's attribution rule), not the
    brief's `Fable 5.1` trailer.
