## How
- `reportsShared.tsx` carries the restyle, so pages keep their hooks and handlers.
  - `ChartCard` is now a `Panel` (`static`) with a `span` prop (default 12). The fixed inline `height` box is kept (a `ResponsiveContainer` needs a sized parent). `help` and `right` sit in the panel head. The banner still sits outside the chart box.
  - `PanelCard` is a flush `Panel` (`span`), so inner tables keep their own padding.
  - `Stat` (reports-local) is a `.panel` with a `StatBlock`; same props as the classic `Stat`, a plain number still counts up. Default `span-3`.
  - `ReportsBalanceTiles` returns four `Stat`s into the caller's `PageGrid`.
- Each report page body is a `PageGrid`. Hidden-when-empty charts hand the row to the survivor (`span-12`).
- Gradient ids use `useId` (`forecast`, `burn`, `past`, `payoff-<id>`), so a second mount cannot collide.
- Larger charts: Cash flow income/net 360 px; Debt history/timeline 300 px; Spending day-of-week 260 px (380 px with a day picked; the panel is `self-start` so its neighbour keeps its height).
- Hub tiles: whole-tile `Link` with `panel panel-link span-4` (not `Panel to=`, so `report-tile-*` ids and the click target stay the whole tile).
