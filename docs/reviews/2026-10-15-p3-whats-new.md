## The first note (verbatim — for the owner to read before any deploy)
- id `2026-10-15` · headline **Clearer spending, bank checks and Amex**
  1. **Spending counts what we know** — This week's spending counts only charges that are clearly spending. A charge that may be a bill, a transfer or a loan payment shows apart as unconfirmed until one of you confirms it.
  2. **Old pending charges stop counting** — A charge still pending more than a week after its date is a hold that never went through. It stays on the list, marked, but no longer counts toward spending or the plan.
  3. **Does H2 match the bank?** — Banking and the dashboard say whether H2's balance matches the bank's. When they differ, Review difference shows what H2 counted and what it can't explain.
  4. **A Banking page that tells the story** — Banking opens with the balance and how sure it is, then what wasn't in the plan: unplanned activity, amount differences and timing differences. Money in and money out show apart.
  5. **One Amex summary for every card** — Amex opens with one summary: what each card owes, then purchases, payments and credits. Pick a card to see only that card. Each row opens its own Details.
- Left out: proposed fixes (owner-only), the What's new feature itself, polish/motion/labels.
