# F2 — evidence-based classification, income direction, correction proposals (2026-10-14)

Branch `fin2/f2-classify` from `fin2/integration` (6a32ec83). Server, avalanche-core, spec and codegen. No web screens (F4).

## Owner's ask
- The week's "counts toward plan" held rows that are not purchases.
- The headline compares confirmed purchases only. Unconfirmed rows are shown apart ("may add up to $X").
- Safe-to-spend stays cautious.
- No silent recategorization of history. Corrections are proposals.

## What changed
### 1. One shared rule — `classifyEvidence` (`lib/avalanche-core/src/evidenceRule.ts`)
- Every outflow → exactly one class. First match wins.
- `confirmed_payment`: transfer, bank noise, debt tag or category, card payment, excluded category, confirmed or tier-2 bill match. These are the spending rule's own facts (`classifyMovement`'s coverage).
- `known_purchase`, household confirmation: a weekly, monthly or unplanned flag, or reimbursable.
- `known_purchase`, unfiled: a purchase-type Plaid primary, or no contrary evidence.
- `known_purchase`, kept as spending: an ATM withdrawal (`TRANSFER_OUT_WITHDRAWAL`), and a Zelle / Venmo / Cash App rail on a `TRANSFER_OUT_ACCOUNT_TRANSFER`.
- `unresolved`:
  - Plaid `LOAN_PAYMENTS` (a card payment is already `card_payment`);
  - `TRANSFER_OUT` (savings, investment, account) or `TRANSFER_IN`;
  - `INCOME`;
  - a bill the forecast only suggests (tier 3, ambiguous, or underpaying);
  - money out filed under an income category.
- A credit is `unresolved` only when filed under an expense category (not a card credit, not a refund). Otherwise the rule returns null.
- Each answer carries `reason`, `evidence[{field, value}]` and, when unresolved, a `suggestion {kind, detail, target}`.
- Plaid primary: read as stored (`pfc_primary`), else from the detailed prefix.
- pending→posted: a posted row with no Plaid category reads its replaced pending row's (`carriedPfcEvidence`, pendingFiling.ts). This is evidence only. The spending rule's `pfcDetailed` is never fed from it.
- A bill suggestion carries from a pending row to its posted row (`billSuggestionsOf`).

### 2. Period breakdown (`GET /money/period-breakdown`)
- New: `known`, `unresolved {amount, nettedByRefunds, count, rows, truncated}`, `excludedPayments {amount, count, rows, truncated}`, `provisional`, and `evidence` on every row.
- `known` = the pool without its unresolved rows, netted per account and never below 0 (`allowanceSpentCents`, the same helper as `actual`).
- `remaining` / `over` / `withinPlan` now read `known`.
- `provisional` = unresolved > max($25, 5% of the plan).
- `actual`, `needsClassification` and `refundFloorAdjustment` are unchanged.
- Unchanged: `/money/position`, safe-to-spend, the spine, the forecast, ways back, the hooks.
- Month: the pool is monthly-flagged rows, and a flag is confirmation, so known = actual.

### 3. Direction guards
- The queue's Accept / Change refuse a category against the money's direction: 409 `{error, code: "direction_conflict", conflict}`, and nothing is written (`queuePickConflict`, categorizer/review.ts).
- Merchant memory is sign-aware. Money in learns and reads `in:<signature>`. Money out keeps the plain signature.
- Legacy plain rows: an income-category row counts as money in's; any other counts as money out's.
- A refund (a card credit or a REFUND word) may still read the purchase's memory.
- Retroactive candidates and apply-retroactively keep to the memory's direction.
- `GET /learned-rules` returns the plain signature plus `direction`.

### 4. Banking review (`GET /banking/review`)
- Rows: `direction`, `classificationIssue` (`inflow_into_expense` / `outflow_into_income`).
- A row's plan and each planned item: `eventKind` (income / bill / debt / hook), `direction`, `expected`, `actual` (magnitudes), `variance` (row − plan, signed in cash: + = more cash), `varianceKind`.
- Buckets and as-planned: `varianceIn` / `varianceOut`, kept apart. Null on a side where no row compares.
- The old direction-blind `difference` / `differenceTotal` are kept as they were.

### 5. `GET /review/proposals` (owner only, writes nothing)
- Window: 120 household days. Largest first, at most 50 items, 20 rows each.
- `loan_payment`: a series of LOAN_PAYMENTS rows (one account, same amount ± max($1, 1%)) is proposed only when exactly one active bill or debt fits:
  - name: a distinctive word of its name or its category's name;
  - amount: a bill within max($25, 25%); a debt at least its minimum.
- `transfer`: `TRANSFER_OUT_SAVINGS` / `ACCOUNT_TRANSFER` rows counted as spending under an expense category, with no P2P rail.
- `bill_match`: an unfiled row the forecast only suggests for a bill.
- `income_category`: a credit under an expense category, primary INCOME, pairing with exactly one income plan by name and amount.
- Each item lists its rows, evidence, action + target, `applyWith` (`categories_change` / `review_mark_matched`) and the occurrence that mark-matched would name.

## Deviations (doubts for the lead)
- The identity is `known + unresolved.amount === actual`, exactly. The brief's `known + unresolved + refundFloorAdjustment` cannot hold together with per-account netting.
  - Example: a card refunds $40 more than it bought, and checking holds $500 of unresolved rows.
  - Known read per account (as `actual` is) gives the card 0, never −40.
  - `refundFloorAdjustment` keeps its meaning: Σ groups + floor = actual.
- `unresolved.amount` = actual − known: what the rows add. The rows sum to amount + `nettedByRefunds`, which is 0 unless a refund on the same account outran its known purchases.
- A flag (weekly / monthly / unplanned) counts as confirmation, even over contrary Plaid evidence. So the month never has unresolved rows.
- A category alone is not confirmation: the savings transfer filed as Subscriptions stays unresolved.
- The P2P rail words (zelle, venmo, cash app, square cash) are the only description words the rule reads. They apply only on a TRANSFER_OUT account / other transfer.
- The breakdown's loan suggestion has no target. Naming the HELOC needs the 120-day series, which only `/review/proposals` reads.
- Debt proposals apply with `review_mark_matched` on the `debt:<id>|<date>` minimum, plus `categories_change` when a debt-linked category exists.
- Live check not done (no production access). The real insurance row may be tier 2 in some states. On fixtures, a tier-3 pair is what makes it unresolved.
