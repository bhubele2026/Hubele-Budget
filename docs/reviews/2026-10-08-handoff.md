## The new experience
- `/` Today: free until payday (never reads high), this week vs your limit, one thing to do, yesterday/today, coming up, debt % paid, handled by H2. "Can we afford something?" .
- Activity: ledger with search, two-tap filing, undo, split, review queue, learned rules, agent trail, findings.
- Plan: weekly limit with its derivation, bills, debt plan (avalanche vs snowball, debt-free range, milestones, log a payment), categories, proposals, wish list.
- Ask: questions answered from your records with linked charges; changes are proposals you approve. Memory you can edit. AI cost page.
- Household: banks (free sync; billable refresh behind a confirm), members, invitations. Recap: verify phone, schedule, preview, test text, history.
- Classic app stays at `/classic` (workbook import still lives there).

## What the agent handles
- Files charges it is sure about (rules, your corrections, recurring bills); queues the rest.
- Watches: bill increase, category pace, shortfall before payday, duplicate charge, stale bank.
- Drafts the 7:00 text from verified numbers; template when the model is off.
- Never moves money, never changes a plan or a budget: it proposes, you approve.

## How filing learns
- Your pick locks the row and teaches a merchant memory. Memory beats rules.
- Undo restores the old category. "Apply to N similar" only when you tap it.
- The model files on its own only after 50 accepted suggestions in 30 days and 9 in 10 judged right, and only if you turn it on.
