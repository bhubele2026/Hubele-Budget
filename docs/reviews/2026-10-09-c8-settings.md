## The area

- One route, seven tabs: **Banks · Household · Data · Automation · Morning text · AI cost · Privacy**.
- The tab rides the query string: `/settings?tab=automation`. Banks is plain `/settings`.
  - Every existing link to `/settings` still lands on Banks: the dashboard's Reconnect, the landing tile, More, the Plaid OAuth return, and the two e2e specs.
  - The shell keys its page entrance on the path, so a tab change does not remount the page. An unsaved allowance or tracker edit survives a look at another tab (tested). Only the tab's content rises in (`section-enter`).
  - `App.tsx`, `routePrefetch.ts` and the route table needed no change.
- Tab bar: plain links with `aria-current`, so Back works and a tab can be linked to.
  - The current tab has a navy underline.
  - On a phone the bar scrolls sideways.
  - Hovering or focusing a lazy tab warms its chunk.
- Every classic hook and handler is the same code and runs whichever tab is open; the bank list keeps its 90 s poll. The render moved; the logic did not.
- The three fold-in tabs are lazy chunks: Automation 13 KB, Morning text 20 KB, AI cost 10 KB.
  - They read the `features` client only; the classic page module never imports it.
  - `Suspense` shows a panel skeleton while a chunk loads.
